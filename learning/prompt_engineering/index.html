Introduction

In this blog I am documenting my learning journey of prompt engineering. I use ChatGPT (GPT-5) for this task.

I initially prompted the chat to create a syllabus for me. I mentioned that I am a developer and comfortable with technical workflows as I prompted the chat to give me the syllabus. The below section lists out my syllabus.

As I am learning, I am using

  • one chat thread as my classroom
  • a second chat as a sandbox to test out prompts
  • a repo as a storage of good prompts. (https://github.com/jac0bmath3w/prompt-engineering.git)

# Phase 1 Lesson 1: Prompt Engineering vs Casual Use.

In casual prompting, I would just be typing what I want without any structure. For example:
- Summarize "Finding Nemo" movie for me.
- Explain the difference between AUC-ROC and AUC-PR.

On the other hand, engineered prompt adds
- role
- context
- constraints
- desired output format
- focus etc.

An example of this would be:
- You are a film critic. Summarize Finding Nemo in 3 sentences, focusing on the main plot. Highlight the emotional themes of family and perseverance. End with a one-line moral lesson.

This prompt includes role (film critic), constraints (3 sentences), focus (main plot, emotional themes), output structure (ending with one-line lesson).
